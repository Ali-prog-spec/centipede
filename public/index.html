DEFEND YOUR GARDEN FROM THE INVASION

Welcome to Centipede Arcade! You are the last line of defense in a vibrant, mushroom-filled garden under attack by a relentless, segmented centipede. Armed with your trusty blaster, your objective is to clear the board and survive as long as possible.

HOW TO PLAY

The game is simple but challenging. As you shoot the descending centipede, any segment you hit will turn into a mushroom, splitting the centipede into two independent parts! This means you have to constantly adapt your strategy as the battlefield fills with obstacles.

DANGERS

The centipede isn't your only problem. Keep an eye out for rogue spiders that jump across the screen. They move fast and can catch you off guard if you're too focused on the swarm above.

PROGRESSION

For every game you play, you can earn tokens, unlock legendary achievements, and climb the global leaderboards. Visit the Store to spend your tokens on exclusive cosmetic skins for your blaster!
